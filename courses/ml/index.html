The progression

Five states, in order

Every level exists to move you from one of these states to the next. If a lesson does not advance you along this line, it was cut.

01
I can say why a score is what it is
Bias and variance are numbers you can measure, the split matches deployment, and the leak has a test that would have caught it.
02
I can pick the metric and the threshold
The metric matches the base rate and the cost, the threshold is a business decision you can defend, and the probabilities mean what they say.
03
I can derive the model I am using
Least squares, the logistic gradient, a Gini split, the SVM dual, the boosting update, PCA's eigenproblem — on paper, on five numbers, then in scikit-learn.
04
I can make it better without fooling myself
Search that beats a grid, resampling inside the fold, a learning curve read for the next action, and an explanation that says what the model actually uses.
05
I can ship it and keep it honest
A forecast with an interval that holds, a serving path with drift monitoring, and a system-design answer with a structure — the model as a product, not a notebook.
